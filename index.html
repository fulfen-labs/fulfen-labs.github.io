Forward deployed engineering

From AI pilot to production, inside your own systems.

Fulfen Labs is a forward deployed engineering practice. We embed with the people who will use a system, build it with their data and constraints, and leave behind something they can prove, run and change themselves.

Start a conversationHow we work

Embed

Work where the problem is

Discovery with the people who do the work, in their tools and their data, before a line of design is written.

Build

Production code, not demos

Grounded models, tools and agents engineered to the customer's security, cost and reliability limits.

Prove

Evidence, then handover

Evaluations and release gates that show the system is better, not just different, and a team ready to own it.
