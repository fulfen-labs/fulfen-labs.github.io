The handbook is the method.

Every engagement follows the six parts of The Forward Deployed Engineer's Handbook, from understanding the role to the human work that decides whether a system is used.

The book names families of technology rather than products, so its advice holds when the products change. So does ours: we work with the cloud and models you already have.

Part 01

The Role

What a forward deployed engineer is: embedded with the customer, writing production code, owning the outcome and carrying what the field teaches back into the practice.

Part 02

The Method

From an ambiguous request to a design that the customer, their security team and an independent reviewer can each challenge and accept, before anything is built.

Part 03

Ground

Models, context and knowledge: the foundation every agent stands on, chosen and tested against the customer's own data and constraints.

Part 04

Orchestrate

Tools, protocols, agents and people working together, and the second job: changing how the customer builds software, so the change outlasts the engagement.

Part 05

Prove

Evidence that the system works, stays safe and can be run by someone else: evaluations, release gates, governance and a clean handover.

Part 06

The Human Side

Trust, sponsors, value, adoption and the conversations that decide whether a working system is actually used.
