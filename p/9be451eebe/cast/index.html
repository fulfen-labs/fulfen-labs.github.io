A real author and an AI cast.

Daniel Vaughan founded Fulfen Labs and wrote The Forward Deployed Engineer's Handbook. Everyone else here is an AI character: an AI-generated person, voice and picture. Each illustrates a role a full forward deployed practice needs, most of them from the handbook. They present the handbook's lessons. They do not do client work.

The AI characters keep the names they first had at Dalex Software, Daniel's project set in a 1987 games studio.
